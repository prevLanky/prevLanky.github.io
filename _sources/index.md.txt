# mygithubpages documentation
Welcome!

## About me
I'm someone who enjoys learning by going beyond the topics I work with professionally.

This site is a personal knowledge base where I document things I research, experiment with, and try to understand in depth.

## How I use this site

When I study a topic, I usually start with the fundamentals and gradually go deeper. I break larger subjects into smaller concepts and investigate individual technologies, terms, architectures, security considerations, and real-world scenarios.

I use AI as a research and writing assistant during this process. It helps me explore ideas, identify areas I need to investigate further, challenge my understanding, and organize information.

The content published here is not intended to be presented as purely human-written material. AI is part of my workflow, but the purpose of this site is to document my own learning and understanding.

Before information is added here, i review, combine, and work through the material myself. I use the site as a structured reference that I can return to, improve over time, and use as context for future learning.

## What you'll find here

The documentation covers topics that I find interesting and want to understand beyond a surface level.

Depending on the subject, this can include:
- Technical concepts and architectures
- Cybersecurity and security engineering
- Vulnerability and CVE analysis
- System design and security considerations
- Notes from hands-on experiments and labs
- Lessons learned

Some pages are introductory, while others go much deeper. The goal is not to create a perfect reference library, but to show how I learn, investigate, structure information, and build technical understanding.

## Why I publish it
I find that documenting something forces me to understand it properly rather than simply recognizing the terminology.
